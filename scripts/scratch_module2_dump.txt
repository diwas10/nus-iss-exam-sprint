=== SLIDE 22 ===
The prominence of a peak measures how much a peak stands out from the surrounding baseline of the signal. The strategy:
Extend a horizontal line from the current peak to the left and right until the line either reaches the window border or intersects the signal again at the slope of a higher peak. An intersection with a peak of the same height is ignored.
On each side find the minimal signal value within the interval defined above. These points are the peak’s bases.
The higher one of the two bases marks the peak’s lowest contour line. The prominence can then be calculated as the vertical difference between the peaks height itself and its lowest contour line.
