•Suppose the highest frequency 
component, in hertz, for a given 
analog signal is fmax. 
•According to the Nyquist-Shannon 
theorem, the sampling rate must be 
at least 2fmax, or twice the 
highest analog frequency 
component.
•If the sampling rate is less than 
2fmax, some of the highest 
frequency components in the 
analog input signal will not be 
correctly represented in the 
digitized output
Source: http://www.writeopinions.com/nyquist-ndash-shannon-
sampling-theorem
Nyquist-Shannon 
theorem
